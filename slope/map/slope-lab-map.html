Slope Lab Map

Every experiment of the bare-slope lab (13–14 October 2026), on one grid. In each, two or more AI sellers set prices round after round in a simulated market, and the question is whether they drift above the fair price without being told to. Rows are the knobs; columns are the experiments by ID. A mark means that knob was turned away from its usual setting in that experiment. Tap a column for what it asked, what we predicted, and what happened.

The top strip under each ID is the verdict on the prediction written before the run. Struck-through IDs are void runs, kept so the journey stays honest. Rows that never change inside the chosen set-up are hidden.
